## Welcome to our corner of the machine.

We’re **KrazyKitty and King Tweak** — two independent builders with different instincts, a shared love of technology, and far too many questions to leave the defaults alone.

One universe glows violet: cyber-neko character, connected systems, Python, security and a terminal full of ideas. The other burns crimson: an obsidian workshop, experimental model architectures, Linux and the urge to take a system apart until it makes sense.

**KITTY × TWEAK LABS** is where those worlds meet. We turn curiosity into code, challenge each other’s assumptions, and work towards experiments someone else can reproduce.

## 💜❤️ KTL // THE SHARED LABORATORY

Our interests overlap across AI, systems and tools. We each bring engineering judgement, creativity and independent projects to the table.

| Shared research space | Questions we want to explore |
|---|---|
| **Models & architectures** | What changes a model’s behaviour, efficiency and capabilities? |
| **Data & tokenisers** | How do provenance, data quality and vocabulary choices affect results? |
| **AMD × NVIDIA** | What transfers between ROCm and CUDA, and what needs different treatment? |
| **Systems & security** | How do we build environments we can understand, observe and protect? |
| **Agents & tools** | How can useful automation remain inspectable and permission-aware? |
| **Evaluation** | What can we measure, reproduce and explain? |

Our roadmap includes **KTL Bench, CrossSilicon, DataForge, DualForge, ReasonKit** and a future **KTL Compute Fabric**. These are proposed or planned programmes; their inclusion here does not claim a completed implementation.

**KittyLM remains KittyLM. TweakTron remains TweakTron.** Independent work keeps its identity and attribution. Joint research is identified explicitly, and third-party work is credited to its original authors.

## Our workshop rules

- **Make it personal.** Our tools and environments should reflect the people building them.
- **Make it understandable.** Follow the system beneath the abstraction.
- **Make it measurable.** Record the environment, method, results and limitations.
- **Make it reproducible.** Keep code, configurations and data provenance connected.
- **Make it secure.** Treat permissions and defensive engineering as part of the design.
- **Make progress deliberately.** Finish meaningful milestones; grow the infrastructure when the work needs it.

Curiosity gives us the idea. Experimentation tests it. Evidence determines what we can claim.
